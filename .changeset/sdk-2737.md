Hidden or unmounted SDK apps close their live connections and presence session about a second after nothing uses them, and reopen them when shown again. `reportPresence` from `@sanity/sdk` now only announces while something subscribes to `getPresence` or `getDocumentPresence`. `useReportPresence` does this for you.
