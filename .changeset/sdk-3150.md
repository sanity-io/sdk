`useUserPreferenceList` reads a long personal preference list, like Vision's saved queries, a page at a time with `loadMore`, and `useUpdateUserPreferenceList` saves or removes one item at a time. Outside React, `userPreferenceList` and `updateUserPreferenceListItem` do the same.
