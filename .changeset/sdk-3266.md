When `useUsers` can't load the next page, the users already shown stay on screen and the failure is on its new `error` field, so the app can call `loadMore` again. The beta `getUsersState`, `resolveUsers` and `loadMoreUsers` in `@sanity/sdk` are deprecated, as is the `userId` option of `useUsers`: read a list of users with `useUsers`, and one user with `useUser`. They're removed in the next major.
